The hand-written books fit this store; another system would need its own. So Mizan also learns laws by itself, from what any system exposes: requests per route, inserts and updates per table, Redis writes. In four minutes of healthy traffic it keeps every pair of flows that moves in a fixed ratio and rises and falls together: 129 laws from 21 flows on Dukkan. A ratio survives load, so a flash sale breaks none of them. A silent failure breaks one, because one side keeps counting while the other stops. Thresholds adapt per law with Adaptive Conformal Inference, and a filling queue counts as lag, not loss.
