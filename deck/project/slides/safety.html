EVERY HANDOVER LANDS IN THE POSTMORTEM, IN ENGLISH AND ARABIC

Letting an agent act is only acceptable with rails. The LLM only proposes; code decides. Every action comes from a fixed catalog of eleven and passes four gates: the safety gate, the validity gate (the action must undo the diagnosis, on that component), a dry run, and a risk check, where a high-risk action waits for the on-call engineer to approve it. An autonomy budget caps each incident at six actions, fifteen minutes and 10,000 SAR; causes outside the store go to a human untouched. After a fix, a no-regression guard, after STRATUS (NeurIPS 2025), undoes it if a learned law that held before it fails on data measured entirely after it.
