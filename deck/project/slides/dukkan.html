Dukkan is a small Saudi online store we built so Nabd has something real to recover: a gateway, auth, inventory, payments, orders, a fulfillment worker and a catalog-sync job, with Postgres, Redis, an order-event queue, a payment provider, a freight carrier, a supplier catalog and simulated shoppers. Faults are injected for real: a config deploy from “CI”, a lock held in Postgres, a memory leak, a network toxic between orders and its database, and silent bugs such as batched inserts that drop orders.
