THE CHALLENGE BRIEF

Five failures. No alerts.

01

Payments silently drop orders

the customer is charged and told “success”

02

A queue loses messages under load

the sender counted it; nobody counts what never arrived

03

A workflow hangs forever

the worker is up and healthy, and stuck on one order

04

A sync job slowly corrupts data

every write succeeds; each run is 1.3% off

05

A repair agent that never escalates

nothing tells it to stop and call a human

NABD HANDLES ALL FIVE, LIVE, ON A RUNNING STORE

All five come straight from the challenge brief, and none of them raises a classic alert: requests succeed, services are up, latency is flat. Nabd was built around them and handles each one live: it detects the damage, fixes the cause, repairs the data, and knows when to stop and hand over to a person.
