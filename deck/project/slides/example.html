logged run, test store

One real incident from our final logged run (eval/results/quick-20261031-0339), on the test store. A bad config deploy makes the orders service drop some saves: every checkout still says success, so monitoring sees nothing. After 21 seconds Mizan sees that the payment ledger has charges with no matching order. Nabd traces one payment through every hop and finds it lost inside orders, right after the deploy. A rollback is high risk, so a person approves it. Then Nabd rebuilds the 39 orders customers paid for (10,671 SAR of test payments) from the ledger, under the order numbers they were already given. It calls the incident recovered only after three clean test checkouts and balanced books: 51 seconds from the fault, zero wrong actions, nothing left charged without an order.
